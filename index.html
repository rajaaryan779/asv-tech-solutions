A pocket handbook for every Maharashtra government employee

Ai प्रशासन is a Marathi-language mobile app built for Maharashtra government employees, bringing salary calculators, service rules and Government Resolutions together in one place.

  • 20+ tools — DA, GPF, pension, gratuity and pay-fixation calculators
  • GR directory — a searchable index of Government Resolutions
  • Rules, in plain language — a guide to Maharashtra Civil Services rules
  • Cloud auto-save & Excel export — every calculation is kept and shareable
  • In-app AI assistant — answers administrative questions on the spot
  • Community help — connects employees across departments for practical guidance

How we work

A process built for momentum

  1. 01

    Discover

    We map goals, users and constraints, then agree on what success looks like and what we're not building.

    Design

    Flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype. You see and feel the product before a line of production code.

  3. 03
